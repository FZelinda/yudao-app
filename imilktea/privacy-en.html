Privacy policy

Your recipes and receipt wallet live on your iPhone. Here’s what happens when you make a drink, pour to a nearby phone, buy a pack or contact us.

Recipes, receipts and preferences

You can play without an iMilkTea account. When you save a receipt, the app stores its identifier, issue time, shop, recipe, base strength, virtual portion consumed and estimated energy of the full recipe on your device. Daily receipt counts and cup equivalents are calculated there. The app also stores preferences such as sound, haptics, controls, backdrop and introduction progress on your device.

The developer has no server that receives recipes or wallets. The app includes no advertising or third-party behavioral analytics and does not read health records. Calories and cup counts refer to simulated drinks.

Motion

The app processes gravity, acceleration and rotation on your iPhone to animate the liquid, ice, milk and pearls. The app does not store your raw motion history or send it to the developer or to nearby devices. Touch controls are available in play settings.

Pouring to a nearby phone

When you choose to send or receive a drink, the app uses the local network to discover nearby iPhones using that feature. It generates and stores a random six-digit table name. Other phones participating in nearby pouring can see that name.

After you select a table, the phones exchange a preview, recipe, base strength, amount, random transfer identifier and an acceptance or decline over an encrypted direct connection. A recipient can see a preview before accepting. Each phone keeps up to 64 recent accepted and 64 recent declined identifiers to prevent duplicate transfers. Your saved receipts and full wallet are not transferred. Leave the pouring screen to disconnect, or turn off Local Network access in iOS Settings.

Purchases, ratings and external references

Apple handles payments, purchase restoration and ratings through the App Store. iMilkTea checks Apple-verified transactions and entitlements on the device to unlock purchased ingredient and illustration packs. The developer does not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password.

If you choose to open a nutrition reference, you visit a third-party website. That website’s own privacy policy applies to the visit.

Apple Privacy Policy

Retention, removal and backups

Saved receipts stay on your iPhone until you remove them from the wallet or choose Delete App to remove its local data. The iOS Offload App option keeps documents and data. Apple manages items purchased through the App Store.

System backups may include app data according to your backup settings. You control those backups. The developer cannot remotely view, restore or remove receipts on your iPhone.

Support and privacy requests

If you contact iMilkTea, the operator uses the reply address, description and attachments you choose to send to handle your request. Correspondence is retained until the issue and necessary follow-up are resolved, subject to legal retention requirements. You may use the same channel to ask about or request deletion of material you sent.

Visiting this website

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub may process IP addresses, access times, browser information and service logs to deliver and secure the pages. These pages include no advertising, analytics scripts or cookies set by the developer.
