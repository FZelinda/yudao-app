Support

Help with your first drink, saved recipes and purchases.

How do I make my first drink?

Open the menu at the milk tea shop, café or tea room. Pick a signature drink, follow the prompts to add its base, mixer and toppings, then shake. For your own recipe, visit Home and choose a base, milk or juice, toppings, sweetness and ice.

How do I lower the drink level?

Hold your iPhone upright, gently tilt it to one side and bring it upright again. For touch controls, choose the hold option in play settings. Hold the screen to lower the level and release to stop.

Where do my receipts go?

Finish a drink to print a receipt. To finish early, tap the cup and choose the option to finish and see the receipt. Save it, then open the receipt wallet on the home screen. Swipe sideways to turn a receipt, down to unfold it or up to fold it. Tap the date to jump to that day.

To remove a receipt, unfold it and tap the remove control in the top corner. The undo button restores it.

How do two nearby iPhones pour a drink?

On the receiving phone, open the nearby pouring option in play settings and choose to receive. On the sending phone, finish making a drink, tap the cup and choose to pour to a friend. Select the table number shown by the receiver. Allow local network access on both phones and keep the pouring screen open. The drink leaves the sender’s cup when the recipient accepts it.

If the table doesn’t appear, bring the phones close together and check iMilkTea’s Local Network permission under Privacy & Security in Settings.

How do I restore a pack?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase. In iMilkTea, open your profile and tap Restore Purchases. Ingredient and illustration packs are one-time purchases. If prices are unavailable, check your connection and reopen the flavor shop later.

What do the cup counts and calories mean?

The energy estimate uses the game’s standard serving, ingredient amounts, sweetness, ice and the share of the drink consumed in the game. It does not measure your real-world diet. Daily cup equivalents add up the virtual portions on receipts saved for that day. Two half-drink receipts add up to one cup.
