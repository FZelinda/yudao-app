Make your own milk tea or coffee, then gently tilt your iPhone or iPad to sip the virtual drink. Find help with recipes, receipts and purchases.

How do I make my first drink?

Open the menu at the milk tea shop, café or tea room. Pick a signature drink, follow the prompts to add its base, mixer and toppings, then shake. For your own recipe, visit Home and choose a base, milk or juice, toppings, sweetness and ice.

How do I lower the drink level?

Hold your iPhone or iPad upright and tilt it gently to one side. The liquid sways and the ice follows. Keep tilting to let the virtual drink run over the rim, then bring the device upright to stop. To play with the device on a table, choose Hold screen in Play settings. Hold the screen to lower the level and release to stop.

Where do my receipts go?

Finish a drink to print a receipt. To finish early, tap the cup and choose the option to finish and see the receipt. Save it, then open the receipt wallet on the home screen. Swipe sideways to turn a receipt, down to unfold it or up to fold it. Tap the date to jump to that day.

To remove a receipt, unfold it and tap the remove control in the top corner. The undo button restores it.

How do I pour a drink for someone nearby?

Use an iPhone or iPad for each person. You can pair two of the same kind or an iPhone with an iPad. On the receiving device, open Me, then Play settings. Open nearby drink sharing and choose “I’ll receive.” On the sending device, finish making a drink, tap the cup, choose “Pour for a friend” and “I’ll pour,” then select the receiver’s table number. Allow Local Network access on both devices.

Once connected, tilt the sending device gently sideways to fill the portion preview. Bring it upright to stop. You can also hold the on-screen pour control. Send the portion when you’re ready. It leaves your cup when the recipient accepts it. If the table doesn’t appear, keep both pouring screens open and bring the devices close together. Connect both to the same Wi-Fi network that lets devices reach each other. A guest network may keep devices isolated. On each device, open Settings, then Privacy & Security and Local Network, and turn on access for Tillysip.

How do I restore a pack?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase. In Tillysip, open your profile and tap Restore Purchases. Ingredient and illustration packs are one-time purchases. If prices are unavailable, check your connection and reopen the flavor shop later.

What do the cup counts and calories mean?

The energy estimate uses the game’s standard serving, ingredient amounts, sweetness, ice and the share of the drink consumed in the game. It does not measure your real-world diet. Daily cup equivalents add up the virtual portions on receipts saved for that day. Two half-drink receipts add up to one cup.

Where can I read the full privacy policy?

Open Me and tap Privacy policy. The app opens the Chinese page when the device’s preferred language is Simplified Chinese. All other languages, including Traditional Chinese, open the English page. You can switch languages at the top of either page.
