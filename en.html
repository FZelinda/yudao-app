A COMPANION AT YOUR PACE

The cat stays free.
More friends, one purchase.

Expense tracking, budgets, savings goals, and charts are free. The Friends Pack unlocks the dog, hamster, bunny, their expense objects, and optional iCloud sync that you choose to enable.

The pack is a one-time, non-consumable purchase with no automatic renewal. Valid purchases can be restored. Apple shows the final price before purchase.

Yudao records your financial life. It does not hold money or make transfers. Budget surplus is an estimate based on your records. Savings jar amounts are entered by you, not verified bank balances.

Your records start on your device.

No registration, bank connection, ads, or analytics tracking. Export a complete JSON backup or a CSV file. Optional iCloud sync uses your own Apple Account’s private database. Sync does not replace a separate backup.
