Xiaoque Shop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

How we handle data

Xiaoque Shop helps you record shop and stall operations. Shop names, products, orders, stock, expenses, customer notes, photos and settings are stored on your device first. The app does not require a Xiaoque account, connect to a bank, include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, or send your ledger to a developer-operated server.

Device storage, exports and backups

The local ledger uses the app's protected storage. You can choose to export JSON, CSV, PDF or a diagnostic summary. Those files are handled by the location, recipients and services you choose. Protect exported files and make a backup before deleting the app.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, the ledger and product photos are stored in the private CloudKit database of your Apple account so your own devices can merge records. Turning sync off does not automatically delete data already uploaded. Apple's handling is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases and subscriptions

Weekly, monthly and annual subscriptions and the lifetime membership are processed by the Apple App Store. Xiaoque Shop uses StoreKit to read product, purchase, validity and revocation states to unlock and restore access. It never receives your card number or Apple Account password and does not use a developer-operated purchase server. Monthly plans include a 3-day trial, annual plans include a 7-day trial, and weekly plans have no trial; the purchase sheet controls the final terms.

Permissions and children

The app does not require Contacts, Location or tracking permissions. Camera and Photos are used only when you choose to scan or select a product image. Xiaoque Shop is not directed to children.

Contact

For support, use “Settings → Help / Privacy / Support” in the app to export a diagnostic summary, then use the contact information on the developer website.
